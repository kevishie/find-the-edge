# Technical Research: Soccer Enrichment Provider Evaluation

## Executive conclusion

Sportmonks is the provisional technical primary for a bounded, non-production field trial across Major League Soccer (MLS), the English Premier League (EPL), Liga MX, and the UEFA Champions League (UCL). It is the highest-ranked candidate under the fixed decision policy below because its public material provides a practical self-service path to test all four competitions while preserving explicit rights and field-depth gates.

This is not purchase or production authority. Production enrichment remains disabled until written terms cover betting-decision support, persistent normalized audit records, derived outputs, AI-assisted synthesis, attribution, and post-termination retention, and until a field trial passes the thresholds in this document.

Sportradar is the premium fallback evaluation path. Its public documentation is stronger on coverage tiers, live update behavior, push recovery, identifiers, missing-player reasons, and advanced data, but price, exact Liga MX depth, licensed properties, rates, history, and permitted use are order-form questions. Stats Perform/Opta remains an analytics-depth option if its pre-match availability feed and contract can satisfy the project. API-Football is rejected for production because its public terms say the service does not provide the license needed to use or publish the data.

SharpAPI remains authoritative for schedules and odds. An enrichment fixture is evidence to map to a SharpAPI-backed canonical event; it does not silently create, delete, reschedule, or reprice one.

## Research method and evidence labels

Research was performed on 2026-08-07 using current public pages published by the providers. All linked pages were retrieved on that date. No archival snapshot or content hash was captured, so mutable pages must be revalidated before procurement and production approval. No credential, paid payload, confidential quote, or non-public contract was used or retained.

Evidence is classified as:

- **Documented:** an official technical page or public term directly describes the behavior.
- **Vendor claim:** an official marketing page asserts scale, accuracy, support, or quality, but the claim has not been independently measured and is not treated as an SLA.
- **Unknown / contract gate:** public material does not establish the exact target-competition behavior or permitted project use. This must stay unavailable or disabled until verified.

Public terms are summarized for engineering evaluation, not interpreted as legal advice. A sales statement does not override an executed order form or license.

Evidence labels apply assertion-by-assertion: endpoint schemas, documented limits, and public terms are **Documented**; product-page statements about breadth, quality, or support are **Vendor claims**; exact target-season field depth, contractual rights, SLAs, premium-feature price, and entitlement are **Unknown / contract gates** unless a table says otherwise. A mixed row inherits the weakest label for the decision it supports.

## Approved scope and architectural constraints

The approved initial soccer competitions are MLS, EPL, Liga MX, and UCL. Friendlies, qualifiers, virtual or esports events, reserves, youth events, futures, novelty markets, and every non-allowlisted competition remain excluded.

The provider must fit the existing capability-port architecture:

- Provider DTOs and provider IDs remain inside the provider adapter.
- Internal event, competition, team, venue, and player IDs remain canonical.
- Cross-provider mappings record provider ID, internal ID, match method, confidence, timestamps, and any manual override.
- Every normalized fact can carry `sourceProvider`, provider entity ID when available, provider timestamp when available, `collectedAt`, verification status, freshness, confidence, and an evidence reference allowed by the contract.
- Missing, stale, conflicting, or unlicensed data is explicit; it is never inferred into a verified fact.
- SharpAPI remains the schedule and odds authority. Enrichment discrepancies enter a reconciliation queue and cannot overwrite canonical kickoff, event status, participants, markets, or prices.
- AI may summarize verified facts and deterministic calculations only. It cannot create facts, calculate authoritative odds, or obscure unavailable inputs.

### Fixed decision policy

The stages have different gates. **Public discovery** requires official evidence that all four competitions can be queried and that core endpoints exist. **Non-production trial eligibility** additionally requires a written trial grant covering the account, properties, developers, environments, fields, and retained evidence corpus. **Production eligibility** requires the dated all-four entitlement, every procurement gate, and every mandatory trial threshold. A failing production rights gate rejects production regardless of points; it does not prevent a separately authorized trial.

Scores use weights fixed before scoring. Each criterion has an explicit anchor: 0 means no supporting official evidence; 1 means partial official evidence or material package/contract uncertainty; 2 means the official public surface is sufficient to execute a bounded trial, even though production rights and field depth may remain gated. Capability cells are supported by the provider scorecards and source lists above; the integration cells are supported by the integration-cost table below; pricing cells are supported by the dated price/support rows. The four-competition score measures discovery, not production entitlement.

## Material unknowns and procurement questions

No candidate may move to production until written answers are attached to the decision record:

1. Does the licensed use expressly include private analysis that supports betting or wagering decisions for the named application and user?
2. May FIND THE EDGE persist normalized facts, source IDs, provider timestamps, hashes/diffs, and immutable report inputs for audit, CLV, reproducibility, and dispute investigation? For how long?
3. May verified provider facts be transformed into derived statistics, rankings, confidence labels, and narrative reports?
4. May those verified facts be supplied to a third-party or first-party generative model solely for synthesis? May prompts, outputs, and audit traces be retained? Is model training excluded?
5. What attribution is required in the private UI, exports, and reports? What upstream rights chain supports the four competitions and fields?
6. What must be deleted, retained, or de-identified at termination? Do previously generated reports and normalized audit evidence survive termination?
7. Are raw response caching and fixture recording allowed in development and contract tests? If so, with what encryption, access, region, and retention limits?
8. Are logos, crests, photos, or headshots separately licensed? The default implementation is text-only and assumes they are not.
9. Which seasons and coverage tiers apply to each of the four competitions for fixtures, venues, rosters, confirmed lineups, predicted lineups, injuries, suspensions, and every requested statistic?
10. What rate limits, concurrency, support response, availability SLA, correction process, planned-maintenance notice, and commercial overage terms apply?
11. What privacy classification, lawful basis, data-processing terms, and deletion duties apply to player injury or health-adjacent fields?
12. Which named users, service accounts, developers, applications, domains, environments, and customer tenants may access the account and data? Are credentials non-shareable, and is each property priced or licensed separately?
13. What immutable response sample or provider-verifiable source evidence may be retained so extraction, empty-state classification, and completeness results are reproducible?

## Authorized field-trial plan

The ADR authorizes planning a trial, not opening an account or accepting terms. A trial begins only after separate approval provides an authorized non-production credential and permits the test data handling below.

### Frozen definitions and scope

- Freeze the SharpAPI source snapshot, trial window, four competition/season mappings, and eligible-event list before querying enrichment data. An eligible event is every SharpAPI canonical event in an allowlisted competition whose scheduled start falls in that window; provider availability cannot change the denominator.
- Freeze a versioned mandatory-capability and required-field manifest per competition/season before collection. Fixtures, venues, rosters, predicted lineups, confirmed lineups, injuries/suspensions, and the named statistics are mandatory for this decision; failure disables all production enrichment rather than silently dropping a capability. “Required-field completeness” uses that manifest, not fields the provider happens to return.
- Predeclare per-gate applicability: event identity includes every frozen event; fixture/venue gates include events not canceled by SharpAPI; roster gates include eligible participating teams; lineup, injury, statistics, and freshness gates include events that reach kickoff. A SharpAPI cancellation is excluded only from inapplicable pre-match gates with its transition and timestamp recorded. Postponements and reschedules retain the same canonical event; if they leave the 90-day window and reduce a competition below 10 applicable matches, the trial fails as insufficient evidence. Provider state never changes applicability.
- Run for at least 14 consecutive days and at most 90 calendar days. Each target competition must contribute at least 10 applicable matches and at least two observed provider corrections, with at least 10 corrections overall; do not substitute friendlies or qualifiers. If a sample is unavailable or a threshold remains unmeasurable by day 90, the trial fails as insufficient evidence and requires a new decision.
- Use a bounded development environment and the provider-neutral capability contracts. Do not add a production adapter or production flag.
- Poll using documented change endpoints and response rate-limit metadata. Stay below 50% of the authorized request budget during ordinary trial collection and record throttles and retries.
- Compare identity and schedule fields to SharpAPI without overwriting SharpAPI records.
- Promotion requires a contract-permitted immutable, access-controlled sample corpus sufficient to reproduce extraction, empty-state classification, and completeness calculations, or provider-hosted/verifiable source evidence with equivalent reproducibility. Normalized observations and hashes alone do not qualify. If neither is permitted, the trial may inform procurement but cannot promote production.
- Retain only that authorized evidence corpus, normalized observations, and permitted minimal test fixtures under the approved retention schedule. Do not retain other raw paid responses.
- Define an observed change as a provider revision/change-feed item with an authoritative provider publication or change-appearance time. Poll at a frozen maximum interval of 60 seconds when plan quotas permit. A normalized diff without an authoritative origin time, or with unresolved clock skew over 60 seconds, is `unmeasurable` and cannot enter the freshness numerator or denominator. Freeze the allowed caller-error and documented-maintenance exclusion taxonomy before the first request; excluded attempts may not exceed 5% of all attempts.
- Count every network attempt after local request validation as an authorized request. Timeouts, malformed success bodies, 5xx responses, throttles, and every retry count against reliability; only frozen caller errors and provider maintenance accompanied by a dated status notice may be excluded.

## Integration and exit risks

- Coverage is field-, competition-, season-, and sometimes match-specific. The adapter must discover capabilities and model absence rather than rely on a marketing coverage count.
- Provider identifiers can merge or change. Mapping history and manual overrides must survive provider replacement.
- Schedule discrepancies can cascade into the wrong match and report. SharpAPI authority plus conflict quarantine is therefore a safety boundary.
- Public update intervals are not freshness guarantees. Measure publication time, provider time, collection time, and display freshness separately.
- “No injury returned” is not evidence of no injury. Empty collections need an explicit coverage/verification state.
- Raw-provider archival in the architecture is conditional. For soccer enrichment, use a permitted evidence reference or normalized hash/diff; never archive paid raw payloads without written rights.
- Provider exit can require deletion. Canonical entities, mapping history, and reports must retain enough non-restricted metadata to explain unavailable evidence, but the contract controls whether provider-derived facts can remain. A contract that prevents the minimum audit trail is incompatible with production use.
