# MLB Prompt Section

Version: 2.0.0

Audit verified starters, handedness offense, arsenal matchup, lineup, bullpen, defense/baserunning, park/weather, travel/rest, and market intelligence. Never decide from the starter alone. Missing hard evidence requires abstention. An unavailable official lineup reduces maturity before 60 minutes to first pitch and requires abstention inside that window. Support canonical `moneyline` and `spread`; display MLB spread as Run Line. The active Find the Edge strategy mechanically prohibits spread even though the sport contract can validate it.
