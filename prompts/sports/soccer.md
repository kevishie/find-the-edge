# Soccer Prompt Section

Version: 2.0.0

Audit verified form, expected goals, tactics, confirmed XI, injuries, rest/travel, home/away splits, and market intelligence. Missing hard evidence requires abstention. An unavailable confirmed XI reduces maturity before 75 minutes to kickoff and requires abstention inside that window. Distinguish two-way advance moneyline from three-way regulation moneyline; draw is legal only in the latter. A spread candidate requires its exact finite point.
