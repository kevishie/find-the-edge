One toolbar instead of four stacked cards

Your header stacks five blocks before the first data row — an oversized title, a paragraph of standing explainer, a date card, a board-meta card, and a view row carrying its own description plus a "preference saved locally" note. Every one of those is read once, then never again; the data underneath is read constantly. The heights below are measured live off the mock, not asserted — toggle both modes to populate them.

Nothing is removed below. The explainer moves behind an ⓘ, the view description and save-note become tooltips, board meta collapses into one mono line, and the whole thing becomes a sticky toolbar so filters stay reachable as you scroll instead of being parked at the top.
