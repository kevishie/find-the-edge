# Evaluation and Learning

Evaluate process, not anecdotes.

## Per recommendation

- Sport/model/calculation version
- Offered and closing price
- Model and market implied probability
- Estimated EV and qualification reasons
- Evidence freshness/completeness
- Decision, stake if manually placed, and outcome
- CLV, profit/loss, and ROI

## Model scorecard

- Calibration by probability bucket
- Brier score and log loss
- CLV distribution and positive-CLV rate
- ROI and units by market, price band, confidence band, favorite/underdog, and decision reason
- No-bet rate
- Recommendation coverage and missing-data rate
- Lineup-change and public-fade performance

Use a minimum sample and confidence intervals before promoting a model change. Never tune from one day, one team, or win/loss alone. Record hypotheses before evaluation and compare versions on frozen historical fixtures when possible.
