# NCAAF Module Specification

Maturity: planned.

Possible markets include moneyline, spread, and totals under explicit strategy configuration. Required categories are team efficiency, returning production, quarterback play, line matchup, explosiveness, havoc, finishing drives, special teams, injuries/opt-outs, travel, rivalry/situational context, and market movement.

NCAAF must apply greater uncertainty than NFL because roster/news quality and team variance differ materially. Before experimental status, define subdivision/team identity, neutral sites, opt-out freshness, provider coverage, grading, and calibration.
